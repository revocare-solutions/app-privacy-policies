Privacy Policy

GlamWell HQ — Platform Administration Console  |  Last updated: October 13, 2026

Summary: GlamWell HQ is an internal platform-administration console used only by authorized Revocare Solutions staff and approved operators. It is not available to the public. This policy covers data handling for the staff who use it.

1. Who Uses This App

GlamWell HQ is restricted to authorized Revocare Solutions personnel and designated platform operators responsible for administering the GlamWell platform. Access is provisioned individually and controlled by role-based permissions.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Operator name, email address, and phone number.
  • Role & Permission Data: Role assignments, access level, and audit trail of actions taken.
  • Platform Data Viewed: Logs of which tenants, facilities, and users were viewed or modified.
  • Authentication Tokens: Securely stored tokens for maintaining your session.
  • Device Information: Basic device metadata for security auditing.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate operators and maintain secure sessions
  • Enforce role-based access to platform data
  • Maintain an audit log of administrative actions
  • Send operational push notifications (e.g. urgent tenant alerts)
  • Protect the platform from unauthorized access and abuse

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Authentication tokens stored using encrypted secure storage.
  • All data transmitted over HTTPS (TLS encryption).
  • Role-based access control enforced on every request.
  • Multi-factor authentication is enforced for operator accounts.
  • Passwords never stored in plain text.
  • Audit logs retained for compliance.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share operator data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

  • Service Infrastructure: Firebase (Google) for push notifications and crash reporting.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Permissions

  • Internet Access: Required to communicate with servers.
  • Push Notifications: Operational alerts for the operator.

7. Data Retention

  • Operator account data retained while the operator role is active.
  • Audit logs retained for a minimum of 3 years for compliance.
  • Account deactivation is managed by Revocare Solutions administrators.

8. Your Rights

  • Access or correct your operator profile
  • Request account deactivation by contacting your Revocare Solutions administrator
  • Withdraw consent for optional data processing

9. Children's Privacy

GlamWell HQ is a workplace application restricted to adult operators. It is not intended for anyone under 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
